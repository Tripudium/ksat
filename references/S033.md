## Concentration audit update, 2026-10-11

The earlier statement-only reading record above is preserved. The
[dated concentration audit](concentration_source_audit_2026-10-11.md)
subsequently checked selected replacement/lifetime steps (pp. 6–7),
the filtrations and root tests (pp. 8–10), occupation and collision
accounting (pp. 11–14), and the cap/tail implications (pp. 15–17).
No gap was identified in those checked steps. The underlying block
comparison, Lemma 2.1, the appendices, Wilson's external theorem, and
formal artifacts were not fully independently audited. This is not
whole-paper or formal verification.

Theorem 1.1, pp. 1–2, and Corollary 5.2, pp. 16–17, give variance and
Chebyshev concentration, not a centered exponential-tail bound.
Proposition 4.3 and Lemma 4.4, pp. 13–14, retain collision environments
and bound their polynomially weighted contributions. The auxiliary
exponential moment controls the incidence count, not the hitting time.

Lemma 6.1 and its proof, pp. 17–18, were independently checked in the
project's exact model: for `k>=3` and `0<c<exp(-2)`, the prefix with
`floor(cn)` clauses is SAT with probability `1-O_c(n^-2)`. Hall matching
and the deficient-variable-set union bound imply a linear mean lower
bound without invoking a limiting threshold. This is compatible with
rare early UNSAT cores; it does not give exponential suppression.
