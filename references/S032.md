## Concentration audit update, 2026-10-11

The earlier statement-only reading record above is preserved. The
[dated concentration audit](concentration_source_audit_2026-10-11.md)
subsequently checked the main upper-bound chain in Sections 2–5.1,
pp. 3–9, including the potential drift, coordinate omission, two
filtrations, root tests, collision averaging, and cap removal.
No gap was identified in those checked steps. This is a scoped manual
audit, not formal verification or a complete review of the paper;
the Wilson-dependent lower bound and any formal artifacts were not
independently verified.

Theorem 1.1, p. 1, and Corollary 5.2, pp. 9–10, concern linear variance
and fixed-level square-root central windows. They do not assert a
two-sided centered MGF bound. Lemma 4.4's exponential moment, p. 8,
is for the root-incidence count, not the hitting time. Proposition 4.3,
p. 7, retains all collision cases, including the deterministic squared
cap on the event of at least two collisions.
