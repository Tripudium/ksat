## Scope, applicability and limitations

Incomplete solver combines unit propagation and progressive local search. Benchmarks are structured SAT Competition 2021-2025 and FCC spectrum-repacking formulas, not a uniform-random-k-SAT comparison. Single-threaded AMD EPYC 7H12 setup; no reproduction. Used to contextualize current incomplete solvers, not to claim a random-SAT champion.
