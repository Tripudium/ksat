## Scope, applicability and limitations

GPU-vs-related-CPU gains, Kissat comparison, Titan RTX/Xeon setup and proof overhead checked. Authors report DRAT checking; this survey did not rerun it. CPU comparisons ran on Xeon E5-2630 nodes, so hardware is not fully matched. Published evidence does not establish GPU superiority on random k-SAT.
