\reportnote{Numerical experiments are not proofs}{%
  Finite tests, plots, floating-point agreement, and symbolic output do not
  establish universal statements. A negative search only concerns the cases
  searched. A suspected counterexample needs exact or rigorously certified
  verification. A computer-assisted proof additionally needs a justified
  reduction, coverage, arithmetic guarantees, and a checkable verification
  procedure.}
