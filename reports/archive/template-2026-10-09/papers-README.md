# Source papers

Store supplied and acquired research papers here with stable, descriptive
filenames, for example `Author_Year_ShortTitle.pdf`. Preserve source originals.
Record author, title, version/date, origin/DOI/URL, and access date in the
[source catalogue](../references/README.md), and add verified bibliography
entries to `reports/references.bib` when cited.

Read relevant papers before relying on their claims. Record precise theorem,
lemma, equation, or page locations and check their hypotheses against the
project's setting. Keep annotations and comparisons in source notes under
`references/`. A paper being present does not mean its contents have been checked.

No papers are supplied with this template.
