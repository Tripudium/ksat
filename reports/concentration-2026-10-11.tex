\reportnote{Main finding}{The two-sided finite-tilt bound proposed in the
research prompt is false in the stated independent proper-clause model,
for every fixed $k\ge3$. Polynomially rare early UNSAT cores defeat
exponential lower-tail control. P005 gives an elementary proof and a
quantitative obstruction even at negative tilts tending to zero.
P006 gives an exact tilted continuation kernel and a conditional
right-tail criterion; its required estimate remains unproved.
Both canonical fragments have independent internal reviews.
No simulation is used as proof, and no general concentration programme
is declared solved.}

\section{The unchanged model and the exact failed target}
For integers $n\ge k\ge2$, each clause independently chooses a uniform
$k$-element subset of the variables and fair independent literal signs.
Whole clauses may repeat and variables may remain unused. On one infinite
sequence put
\[
 F_m=\bigwedge_{i=1}^m C_i,\qquad F_0=\mathrm{True},\qquad
 T_n=\min\{m\ge0:F_m\text{ is UNSAT}\},\qquad \mu_n=\mathbb ET_n.
\]
Then $\Pr(F_m\text{ is SAT})=\Pr(T_n>m)$ exactly. The prompt asked whether,
for each fixed $k\ge3$, finite constants $C_k,b_k>0$ could satisfy
\begin{equation}
 \log\mathbb E e^{\theta(T_n-\mu_n)}
 \le \frac{C_k n\theta^2}{2(1-b_k|\theta|)}
 \quad\text{for every }n\ge k,\quad
 |\theta|<1/b_k<-\log(1-2^{-k}).
 \label{eq:original-target}
\end{equation}
This precise target is refuted; it has not been silently replaced by
a conditioned ensemble or a one-sided claim.

\paragraph{The obstruction in two steps.}
Write $B=2^k$. An elementary clause-to-variable matching argument gives
\[
 \mu_n\ge dn,\qquad d=\frac1{8e^2},
\]
uniformly for $n\ge k\ge2$. Meanwhile, the first $B$ clauses may comprise
every sign pattern on one fixed set of $k$ variables. This event has
probability
\[
 p_{n,k}=\frac{B!}{[B\binom nk]^B}
 \ge \frac{B!}{B^B}\,n^{-kB}
\]
and forces $T_n=B$. Therefore for every $s>0$,
\begin{equation}
 \log\mathbb E e^{-s(T_n-\mu_n)}
 \ge s\mu_n-sB+\log p_{n,k}
 \ge sdn-O_k(\log n)-sB.
 \label{eq:summary-obstruction}
\end{equation}
For arbitrarily small fixed $s$, this grows linearly in $sn$, whereas
\eqref{eq:original-target} demands a bound quadratic in $s$ with constants
uniform in $n$. Section~\ref{sec:canonical-obstruction} gives all constants,
boundary cases, and the contradiction.

\paragraph{Even a very small negative tilt is enough.}
For a sufficiently large fixed $A=A_k$, at
$s_n=A\log n/n$ the centered log-mgf is at least a positive multiple of
$\log n$, while the proposed right-hand side tends to zero.
Moreover, some $u_n\in[0,s_n]$ satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_{-u_n}(T_n)
 \ge c_{k,A}\frac{n^2}{\log n},\qquad c_{k,A}>0.
\]
This is an analytic existence result about the tilted variance, not a
numerically located tilt or an estimate of $\operatorname{Var}(T_n)$.

\paragraph{What this does not refute.}
For $k\ge3$, polynomially rare far-left events can coexist with an
$O_k(n)$ ordinary variance and $O_{k,\eta}(\sqrt n)$ fixed-confidence
windows. The proof refutes neither those claims nor a positive-tilt-only
bound. It also does not identify $\mu_n$ to within $\sqrt n$ of
$\alpha_k n$. The $k=2$ control has its separately known
$n^{2/3}$ clause-window scale \citep{bbckw2001}; the obstruction's validity
at $k=2$ is not a square-root-window assertion.

\section{What the actual sources establish}
\label{sec:source-audit}
The dated source audit reads the actual S001, S032 and S033 PDFs, compares
their models and proof mechanisms, and checks the current primary
versions. Fresh PDFs matched the retained hashes; no relevant correction
notice appeared in the checked locations. This is a targeted audit,
not exhaustive literature coverage or formal verification.

\begin{itemize}
 \item S001, \citet[Theorem 1.3, p.~2]{carenini2026}, states
 $\operatorname{Var}(T_n)=O_k(n^{1+2/k})$ for fixed $k\ge3$,
 with Chebyshev concentration. Equations (2.4)--(2.5), p.~5, give
 the fresh remaining-time drift and the globally forced-coordinate
 killing probability.
 \item S033, \citet[Theorem 1.1, pp.~1--2; Corollary 5.2,
 pp.~16--17]{oaVariance}, states an $O_k(n)$ upper variance bound
 for $k\ge4$ and an $O(n\log n)$ one for $k=3$.
 Its Lemma 6.1, pp.~17--18, gives a sparse-prefix SAT estimate by
 Hall matching. That argument was independently checked; our P005
 includes a self-contained, deliberately weaker uniform mean bound.
 \item S032, \citet[Theorem 1.1, p.~1; Corollary 5.2,
 pp.~9--10]{oaVariance3}, states linear variance also for $k=3$.
 The scoped audit checked the main upper-bound chain in Sections
 2--5.1, including its collision accounting, without identifying a gap.
 Its Wilson-dependent lower bound and formal artifacts were not
 independently verified here.
\end{itemize}
None of these sources states \eqref{eq:original-target}. Their exponential
estimates concern either the far-right first-moment tail beyond a large
linear cap, or auxiliary root-incidence counts. S032 and S033 average rare
collision environments in second-moment estimates, rather than deleting them by
conditioning. That suffices for the claimed variance calculations but
does not control their contribution under a negative hitting-time tilt.
The S001 external covering/threshold dependencies and the S033 block
comparison and appendices were not fully audited.

To see the confidence-level distinction explicitly, an upper bound
$\operatorname{Var}(T_n)\le V_k n$, if established, gives only
\[
 \Pr\{|T_n-\mu_n|\ge\sqrt{V_k n/\delta}\}\le\delta,
 \qquad 0<\delta<1,
\]
by applying Markov's inequality to $(T_n-\mu_n)^2$.
This yields a square-root scale at fixed $\delta$ but not
the exponential-confidence dependence requested in
\eqref{eq:original-target}. This displayed conclusion is conditional
on the variance bound, not a new proof of the source theorem.

\paragraph{Comparison with the supplied geometry papers.}
In S003, \citet[Sections 8.2--8.6, pp.~223--227]{lotz2026}, the Steiner
mixture identifies a family of tilted log-concave measures. Lemma 8.3's
thermal-variance identity and Fact 8.4's variance-of-information bound
give control throughout the tilt family. In S002,
\citet[Appendix E.3, pp.~290--293]{almt2014}, Gaussian logarithmic
Sobolev control combines with the squared-projection gradient identity.
The missing SAT analogue is quantitative control under the appropriate
tilted law. P005 now proves that a uniform two-sided linear-variance
analogue cannot hold. This is more precise than merely lacking a
discrete convexity theorem.

\section{Methods compared and the remaining research choices}
The following comparisons keep the exact SAT observable visible.
They record proof requirements rather than treating a convenient
auxiliary statement as established.

\paragraph{Covering and matching.}
The observable is the first covering of all assignments by clause
falsification sets. A clause-to-variable matching ensures sparse
prefixes are SAT; an explicit small covering gives early UNSAT mass.
These two elementary inputs already decide the proposed target
negatively. They give no sharp typical transition location.

\paragraph{Martingales, stopping times and change of measure.}
The observable is the remaining absorption time from a solution set.
Section~\ref{sec:canonical-tilt} gives its exact tilted kernel and
variance-occupation identity. This isolates a possible positive-tilt
estimate (H+). The first unproved step is to bound its occupation
term uniformly in $n$ for fixed $k$, not to assert clause independence.
The state space is finite but enormous; the identity is not an efficient
algorithm for computing these quantities.

\paragraph{Clause omission, Efron--Stein and entropy methods.}
The source omission arguments use independent ordinary clauses and
root tests, together with polynomial bounds on collision events.
Their stated control is untilted and second-order. A tilted version
requires a new law and exponentially weighted exceptional-event
estimates. P005 rules out completing this programme with the same
two-sided target. Deterministic unit bounded differences for $T_n$
are unavailable because a single replacement can postpone absorption
arbitrarily far along an individual sequence.

\paragraph{Discrete integral geometry, overlaps and spectral methods.}
P003 gives exact cube-intersection and projection identities.
Nonemptiness cannot be replaced by Euler characteristic or recovered
from a face vector alone; the documented examples also defeat
universal face-profile log-concavity. Richer overlap or projection
profiles remain candidates, but require a concentration/correlation
estimate tied to exact nonemptiness. No such estimate is proved here.

\paragraph{Soft energies and partition functions.}
For a fixed $m$-clause formula, let $U$ be the minimum violation count and
$Z_\beta=\sum_x e^{-\beta H_F(x)}$.
Replacing one clause changes $U$ by at most one and $\log Z_\beta$ by
at most $\beta$, but
$-\beta U\le\log Z_\beta\le n\log2-\beta U$.
The missing bridge is resolution of the atom $U=0$. Concentration at
the available proxy scales does not distinguish $U=0$ from $U=1$.

\paragraph{Next discriminating step.}
Investigate (H+) as an explicitly one-sided candidate, while testing
which parts of the clause-omission exposure remain valid under the
kernel in P006. For lower tails, any proposed all-confidence bound
must include exceptional mass compatible with P005. Removing only
the exhibited smallest cores is not known to suffice: larger
obstructions remain. Conditioning or trimming would be a separately
specified problem and require a transfer back to the original law.
No such transfer, exceptional-tail upper bound, or positive-tilt
bound is claimed here.

\section{Handoff and interpretation limits}
P005 settles the stated two-sided target negatively, using an exact
event family and elementary inequalities. P006 supplies a correct
tilted-law reduction rather than a proof of concentration. The first
remaining concentration gap is (H+), or a precisely stated alternative
that accounts for exceptional lower-tail mass. The clause model and
the original failed target remain recorded unchanged.

The source audit is separate from the internal reviews of P005/P006.
No external theorem is labelled formally verified, no novelty is
asserted, and no numerical fit or unsuccessful search is used as proof.
No new scientific experiment was needed or performed. The E002 batch
workflow samples independent fixed-density formulas. Its SAT probabilities
estimate the survival function $\Pr(T_n>m)$, but it does not record coupled
clause paths or individual hitting-time samples.

This dated report supplements the preserved feasibility report; it does
not rewrite that report's earlier proposed target as if it had never
been considered. A successful build and visual inspection validate
presentation only.
