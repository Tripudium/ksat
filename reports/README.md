## Build

Run `make report` from the project root. The build uses `latexmk` and
`pdflatex` with BibTeX, New PX fonts, PGFPlots and the packages in
[preamble.tex](preamble.tex). It writes `build/main.pdf` and copies it to
`output/pdf/report.pdf`.

The active article consists of `sections/survey-*.tex`, with the canonical
P001 and P002 arguments included from `../proofs/`.
The analytic figure is generated by PGFPlots in Section 3; it is not based
on a solver experiment. Old template section files are unused and unmodified.

## Provenance and review

The supplied papers and acquired originals are catalogued with checksums.
Published results, preprint claims and measurements are explicitly distinguished.
No solver benchmark or Lean artifact was run for the initial survey. P001 and P002 remain `proof-written`
until independently reviewed. The build and visual inspection concern document
quality, not mathematical verification.

[Revision checks](QA_2026-10-11.md) and the dated source manifest record the
current version. The [v1.0 delivery checks](QA_2026-10-10.md), dated PDF and
dated source bundle remain available. The original unconfigured report is retained under
`archive/template-2026-10-09/`.

## Version 1.1 changes

Section 2.1 adds the sharp m < 2^k satisfiability guarantee, a counting proof,
and the k=2 example. Section 2.3 defines the common clause sequence, explains
its dependence across clause counts, and relates the first UNSAT time to
satisfiability probabilities. Existing section numbers are preserved.

## Discrete-geometry and computational follow-up

The separate [10-page feasibility note](output/pdf/geometry-feasibility-2026-10-11.pdf)
assesses proof transfers and ALMT-style diagrams. Its
[LaTeX source](geometry-feasibility-2026-10-11.tex) includes canonical P003/P004,
with separate internal reviews. A001 records the unproved research targets;
E001 retains the limited CPU calibration, including timeouts and witness checks.
