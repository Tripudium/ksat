# Original source PDFs

The supplied originals are `TR26-229.pdf`, `iau005.pdf`, `sharp-phase.pdf`,
and `montanari-phase.pdf`. They are preserved unchanged. The last file is
about neural-network feature learning, not random k-SAT.

Additional primary-source PDFs support the survey. Bibliographic identities,
versions, retrieval links, checksums and precise reading locations are in
[the source catalogue](../references/README.md). Filename years may reflect
an initial manuscript rather than final publication; use the catalogue.
Annotations are separate from original source files.

`Cen_2025_FastFourierSAT_published.pdf` is the published version used in the
survey; `Cen_2025_FastFourierSAT.pdf` retains an earlier arXiv version.
`Kaporis_Kirousis_Lalas_2003.pdf` is an early manuscript; the journal
continuation was consulted online and is distinguished in S010.

Third-party papers retain their original copyright and licensing terms.
